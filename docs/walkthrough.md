# Walkthrough

How to present each demo, one paragraph per module in the order a presenter walks them. The app shows this page under Presenter run sheet in the sidebar footer. Every number on screen is a count of seed records or a seed-computed amount labeled example data.

## Getting around

Clone, `npm install`, `npm run dev`, open http://localhost:3000. The app opens on Demo home: one card per module, grouped by department (Family Office AP, Industrial AP), each with one sentence on what it does and a Start button that opens the module's first screen. Demo controls live there too: Reset all demos, and Reset this demo on every card. Every screen opens with its narration under the title, three short columns the presenter reads from: what happens today, what happens with this screen, and what that protects. Under that sits a "Connects to" strip naming the systems that screen would touch (Monday.com, Yardi, Avid, the parcel database, the legacy property system, the shared drive, the provider portals) and whether it reads from them, hands something back, replaces them, or leaves them alone. Nothing is connected in the demo, and the strip says so.

Navigation is the left sidebar, collapsible to icons. Selecting a module expands its screens beneath it, in the order the work happens, and opens the first one; only one module is expanded at a time, and the sidebar remembers which across reloads within the browser session. The slim top bar carries the breadcrumb (department, module, screen; every level is a link, and the department link filters Demo home to that group) and, on Family Office screens, a Property Owner Lookup button that opens the lookup as a side panel from anywhere. The sidebar footer has Reset this demo for the module that is open and Presenter run sheet, which opens this walkthrough.

Keys: `[` and `]` move between the screens of the open module, in order. `a` hides or shows the narration on any screen (Escape hides it); the choice is remembered for the browser session. Inside the utility demos: in Match bills to meters, Enter accepts the suggestion, N skips, M opens the full picker, U undoes the last action; in Confirm which account a payment matched, 1, 2, 3 pick a candidate.

Routes are `/<department>/<module>/<screen>` with the department slugs `family-office-ap` and `industrial-ap`. Nothing in any demo is really sent anywhere: Yardi, Avid, the property tracker, email, and the utility portals are stand-ins or seed lists, import files are downloaded and never imported, and every "paid" step is a simulation.

## Family Office AP

### Property Owner Lookup

Open Look up an owner and type a parcel number (P-12003) or a street address (45 Hillcrest Dr Unit 2, Peoria IL): one owner record comes back with its entity code and cash account, or the parcels that could be meant, or nothing. The same lookup runs inside AP Inbox on every invoice and opens as a side panel from the top bar on every Family Office screen. Owner to entity map shows every owner with its entity code, cash account, and parcel count; it is example data.

### Multi-property invoice splitter

The sidebar entry opens the AP Inbox splitter on I-0007: the photo of nine lots beside nine lines at 50.00 each, split equally because the invoice gives no prices, sum 450.00 green, one lot flagged as not in the property list. Click Create property on that line, then Submit. Then open I-0012: ten sections with stated amounts and different ledger accounts per line, sum 6,988.40 green. Say: when the amounts are on the invoice we never split; when they are not, we do, and the total always has to add up.

### AP Inbox

Open This week's arrivals: 26 arrivals, each tagged Email, Tracker approved, Utility portal, or Mail scan, every one with a draft already behind it. Open I-0005 in Review an invoice: the document on the left, the ten fields on the right, each showing where it came from; the vendor matched to the master at 0.94 despite the short name, the ledger and cash account from the vendor's history, the entity resolved from the address through the parcel registry, the post month defaulted; click Submit. Open I-0011, point at the Tracker approved badge and its card reference, and Submit. Work the splitter items I-0007 and I-0012 as above. Go to Needs a person and work the cards: Create vendor on I-0003, search P-10777 and Map to this parcel on I-0018, Skip the duplicate on I-0009, Choose this owner on I-0026; then open I-0003, pick its ledger account and Submit, and open I-0019, Confirm amount and Submit. Back on This week's arrivals click Submit all ready drafts; on Approve click Approve all as the approver; on Ready for Yardi show 24 invoices and 44 lines, Download import file, the 15-column preview, then Mark batch paid. Open Property tracker updates: a message on every property card, and the filter "came by email, not in tracker before" shows I-0015. Finish with Reset this demo, which reloads the week with all 26 items new.

## Industrial AP

The three utility modules (Utility bills to Yardi, Meter register, Paid-or-not reconciliation) are one system with one seed and one demo state: resetting any of them resets all three. The seat switch (accounting lead, AP operator) sits in their module bar. The full presenter beats are in `docs/run-sheet.md`.

### Utility bills to Yardi

Open Capture this month's bills and press Run Aug 2026: the run panel ticks received by route, fields read, matched, surfaced by bill, unmapped, meter on bill differs, duplicate blocked, unusual amount, and the invoice number column fills as the bills are read, built the way the team builds it by hand from the last five digits of the account number and the due date; export stays disabled while the duplicate is open. Go to Match bills to meters and work the queue with the keys: Enter on the suggested meter for the Site B house-meter bill, S to confirm the swap on the printed-meter mismatch, F to drop the second copy of the duplicate, 1 (vacant but in use) on the unusual amount, and U once to show undo. Back on Capture this month's bills, Export to Yardi downloads one zip: a 13-column headerless file per property per provider, water bills as three rows under one invoice number, plus the bill-backs file. Press Yardi import confirmed: the paid column fills itself.

### Meter register

Open Every meter and its account for Site A: house meters at the top, unit meters below, each with its account number and holder. Open One meter's history for unit 12, then unit 07: unit 12 vacant since June with the landlord account active from the day after and no ledger line for August; unit 07 occupied since April with the landlord's account never closed, bill-back four months so far, Transfer needed on the row. Say: who should have paid July, and who should have paid August; today the workbook cannot tell you that. The provider account list import and the site visit results, reached from Which bills are paid, fill the register: No account on record falls, blank meter numbers fall, carried-over rows verify as tenant-paid.

### Paid-or-not reconciliation

Open Which bills are paid: the portfolio strip for August with counts per status, then Site A's One property, month by month: the red cell (Unpaid, the shut-off risk) and the dark grey cell (No account on record) side by side; click any cell for why this status (the rule, the rent roll on the 15th, the account on the 15th, the ledger lines, the flags). Read the month stepper: July closed, August this month, September not yet. After the utility export and Yardi import confirmed, the Site B county water master reads Paid because its three lines sum. Confirm which account a payment matched: the legacy line whose four-digit suffix two Site A accounts share; pick the candidate, and say that after this month's export September will have nothing here. Payments without a meter lists the ledger lines that cannot be placed. Bill-backs, transfers and calls: bill back unit 07, draft a transfer for a quiet-site row (nothing is sent), generate the checklist for Site B, mark a provider call; Files and notes produced shows what came out. The tracker as kept today: the team's tracker as it is kept by hand, one tab per property, next to the rent roll tab and the discrepancy list, with the manual steps beside the demo's. Step to September and read the deltas per count and the contributions per mechanism.

### Invoice description writer

Open Receive today's invoices and press Receive invoices: one day's folder of 30 invoices arrives, and the summary reads 30 received, 27 ready to paste, 1 needs manual entry, 2 routed to utilities. Open INV-3007 in Write the invoice description: the invoice on the left, the fields read from it on the right, and the Description card reads "Coral Ridge Roofing roof leak repair PR PR-12 2026-08-24 to 2026-08-26"; press Copy description, then point at the Suggested code card (6320 Roofing repairs, coded to this 14 of 16 times, strong) and press Used as is. Move to INV-3012, where the description says "dates not on invoice" instead of inventing dates; to INV-3019, a new vendor with no history, so the code card says so and stays blank; to INV-3021, the logo invoice the reader could not read, flagged for manual entry with the missing fields listed; and to INV-3030, a weak 9 of 15 suggestion with the alternative listed: press Corrected, pick 6520, Record correction, then open How this vendor was coded before for Tidewater Plumbing to show the correction recorded this session. The house description order shows the template that wrote every description. Back on Receive today's invoices, point at INV-3025, routed with the note "utility, handled by the capture pipeline". Close with Reset this demo: the inbox empties, the session line clears, and the script replays identically. Example data throughout.

### Vendor Creator to Avid

Open Create the vendor once in Yardi: two plain lists, 119 vendors in Yardi and 112 in Avid, with the gap counter reading 5 between them. Fill the form with "Marlin Bay Cleaning", an address, active, and press Create vendor in Yardi; that is the only time anyone types it. Go to Run the nightly sync to Avid in assisted mode and press Run nightly sync: compared 120, skipped exact 110, staged 6, held 2, skipped inactive 2; press Download import file (6 rows) and point at the six task lines. Flip the toggle in the module bar to Automatic, run again, and show the Avid list growing with rows marked synced. On Decide the near matches open the PW Maintenance pair (same normalized name, same address, suffix difference) and press Same vendor, link; open the Tidewater pair (similar name, different address, different tax digits) and press Different, create. On Catch unknown payees on arrival press Receive invoices: INV-5007 is first-seen at day zero with a shortcut that opens the form pre-filled, and INV-5009 is a near match that has waited seven days. Close on the fixed line at the bottom of the sync screen, then press Reset this demo. Nothing is sent to Yardi or Avid; both masters are seed lists.
